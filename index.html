Apostille & authentication

We help you prepare your documents correctly

Apostille

Foreign educational documents may require an apostille, depending on the issuing country.

Consular authentication

Where apostille does not apply, consular authentication may be required instead.

Documents that are not in Korean or English generally require a notarized Korean or English translation. Our team can provide guidance on the appropriate preparation process.

!
Document requirements are determined by the university and relevant authorities. Additional documents may be requested during the review process.

Career & employment pathway

From education to career

Our program aims to support students beyond university admission by providing appropriate career-development opportunities, including career counseling, vocational education, job information, work-based learning, internship opportunities, and employment connections.

Employment is not guaranteed
!
Part-time employment, internships, work-based learning, and post-graduation employment must comply with Korean immigration regulations, student residence status, required permissions, reporting requirements, academic requirements, employer requirements, and applicable laws.

Employment with an agricultural cooperative, the National Agricultural Cooperative Federation/NongHyup-related organization, or another partner employer is subject to separate screening and an employment contract. Any wages or employment conditions are determined under the applicable employment arrangement.

Ethical recruitment commitment

What we do not promise

We do not promise

  • Guaranteed university admission
  • Guaranteed visa approval
  • Guaranteed employment
  • Guaranteed salary
  • Guaranteed scholarship
  • Guaranteed permanent residence
  • Guaranteed immigration status

We do not support or participate in

  • False documents or document alteration
  • Proxy examinations
  • Bribery or kickbacks
  • Illegal employment or illegal stay
  • False advertising
  • Undisclosed fees

Every student application must be based on genuine information and authentic documents.

Document authenticity

Your documents must be genuine

Students must confirm the authenticity of the documents they submit. Our preliminary verification process may include:

  • Comparing documents with originals
  • Reviewing issuing institutions
  • Checking inconsistencies
  • Requesting additional evidence where appropriate
!
If falsification, alteration, or material omission is discovered, the matter may be reported through the appropriate program/university process. Admission may be suspended or cancelled according to university rules and applicable law.
Privacy & data protection

Your information matters

To process an international student application, certain information may need to be shared between authorized parties in Ethiopia and Korea. We collect and use only information necessary for recruitment, admission, immigration/visa processing, academic administration, student support, and career support.

Students should provide explicit consent where required for the collection, use, disclosure to authorized parties, and cross-border transfer of personal information. Personal information should only be accessed by authorized personnel and securely handled according to applicable requirements.
