About EGG

Guidance from someone who has walked the path.

EGG was founded by Eshetu Gudeta Geresu to give Ethiopian students clear, honest guidance at the moments that matter most: choosing a field of study, applying to the right institution, and preparing for life on a new campus. We work with each student individually, taking the time to understand their goals, grades, and circumstances before recommending a path.

Founder & Lead Consultant — Eshetu Gudeta Geresu

Why families choose EGG

We sit down with every student, not just their transcript. Recommendations are built around their goals and budget, not a one-size-fits-all list of schools.

What we don't do

We don't promise guaranteed admission or make decisions for the student. Our job is to prepare them to make an informed choice, and to make the process less overwhelming.

Who we work with

High school graduates planning their next step, students transferring programs, and families weighing local study against studying abroad.

How It Works

Our Approach

What guides every recommendation.

Honesty first

We tell students when a goal is realistic and when it needs adjusting — before they spend money finding out.

Individual attention

Every plan is built for one student, not copied from the last one who walked in.

Clear timelines

Deadlines for tests, applications, and visas are tracked so nothing is missed.

Ongoing support

The relationship doesn't end at acceptance — we check in through the first term.
